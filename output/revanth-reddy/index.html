Key insights

  • Revanth's week was defined by a high-profile national moment: the NITI Aayog Governing Council with PM Modi, where his M-6 Task Force and education-first pitch dominated coverage.
  • Bharat Future City was the second engine of positive sentiment - the FCDA launch and 'rival Tokyo/New York by 2034' framing drove an aspirational narrative.
  • Unlike Bandi Sanjay's controversy-driven week, Revanth's negativity was diffuse civic grievance (power, sewage, water) rather than a single scandal.
  • His highest-reach post - meeting PM Modi at about 28K views - outperformed everything else, showing national-stage content travels furthest.
  • Opposition attacks were present but never coalesced into one dominant hostile cluster, leaving net sentiment more balanced than negative.
